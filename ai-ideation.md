# AI Interaction Audit
AI was used to generate alternative solutions, compare feasibility, and stress-test the camera-based concept.

## Key decisions
- **Adopted:** camera-based vehicle detection and dashboard.
- **Rejected:** mobile-only parking database because it requires unavailable large-scale data.
- **Rejected:** automatic penalty generation because AI detections require verification.
- **Corrected:** any claim of guaranteed congestion reduction; this must be measured in real deployment.

## Stress-test findings
Lighting, weather, occlusion, camera angle, false detections, privacy, and threshold calibration are important limitations.
